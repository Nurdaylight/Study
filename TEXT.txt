Once upon a time there was a little girl.
The little girl lived in a small village.
The little girl was very kind and very good.
Everyone in the village liked the little girl.

The little girl had a mother.
The little girl had a grandmother.
The grandmother lived in a house in the woods.

The little girl wore a red hood.
She wore the red hood every day.
Because of the red hood, people called her Little Red Riding-Hood.

One day, the mother said:
“Little Red Riding-Hood, go to your grandmother.
Your grandmother is sick.
Take this food to your grandmother.”

Little Red Riding-Hood said:
“Yes, mother. I will go to my grandmother.”

So Little Red Riding-Hood walked.
She walked through the woods.
She walked and she walked.

In the woods, she met a wolf.
The wolf was a bad wolf.
The wolf wanted to eat Little Red Riding-Hood.

The wolf said:
“Where are you going, little girl?”

Little Red Riding-Hood said:
“I am going to my grandmother’s house.”

The wolf thought:
“I will go to the grandmother’s house.
I will eat the grandmother.
Then I will eat the little girl.”

The wolf ran fast.
The wolf ran very fast.
The wolf came to the grandmother’s house.

The wolf knocked on the door.
The wolf went inside.
The wolf ate the grandmother.

The wolf put on the grandmother’s clothes.
The wolf got into the grandmother’s bed.
The wolf waited.

Soon, Little Red Riding-Hood came.
She knocked on the door.
She went inside.

She looked at the bed.

“Grandmother,” she said,
“what big ears you have.”

The wolf said:
“All the better to hear you.”

“Grandmother,” she said,
“what big eyes you have.”

The wolf said:
“All the better to see you.”

“Grandmother,” she said,
“what big teeth you have.”

The wolf said:
“All the better to eat you.”

The wolf jumped.
The wolf ate Little Red Riding-Hood.
